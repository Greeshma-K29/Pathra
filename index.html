Privacy

Pathra stores your profile, emergency contacts, activity history and Guardian AI chat locally in this browser. Location is only requested with your consent and is used to power Safe Route, Traffic Watch, Green Trail and SOS. Nothing is sent to a government or police database.

Safety score

Safety scores and traffic estimates are heuristic calculations from public map data, weather and time of day. They are not official police, crime, CCTV or government safety data, and should be used as one input among many.
